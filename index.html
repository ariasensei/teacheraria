📋 Terms & Conditions

Our Commitment to You

🔒 Privacy Statement

Your privacy, trust, and learning development are foundational to our partnership. At Aria's Learning Hub, we maintain the highest standards of confidentiality and data protection to foster a safe, supportive learning environment.

Personal Information

Any personal information you entrust to us—including your name, contact details, academic background, and learning goals—is handled with utmost care and used exclusively for educational purposes. This information enables us to create a personalized learning experience tailored to your unique needs and aspirations.

Learning Progress & Records

Your learning progress, assessment results, and performance evaluations are treated as strictly confidential information. These records are maintained solely to track your academic growth and refine your personalized learning plan. We never share your educational data with third parties without your explicit consent.

Communication Standards

Your contact information is used exclusively for educational purposes, including class schedules, assignment distribution, constructive feedback, and session coordination. We respect your communication preferences and use your details only within the scope of your learning journey with us.

Recording & Session Documentation

Some sessions may be recorded to support your learning review and enable us to continuously improve our instruction. You will receive advance notice before any recording occurs. All recordings are securely encrypted and accessible only to you and your instructor. You may request to opt out of recordings at any time.

Your Data Rights

  • ✓ Access: Request and review your personal learning data anytime
  • ✓ Correction: Request updates to any inaccurate information
  • ✓ Deletion: Request removal of your data upon conclusion of your learning journey

📝 Service Agreement

1. Scheduling & Class Coordination

• Available schedules are provided transparently before any enrollment commitment

• We recommend booking sessions at least 24 hours in advance to ensure optimal preparation

• A consistent study schedule accelerates your learning progress and maximizes results

2. Class Packages & Usage Terms

• Purchased class packages are reserved for the enrolled student and are non-transferable

• Special promotional packages may include specific conditions—these will be clearly communicated

• Your class distribution schedule must be finalized before your first session begins

3. Cancellation & Rescheduling Policy

• To cancel or reschedule, please provide at least 3 hours' notice prior to your scheduled session

• Cancellations with insufficient notice will result in the class being deducted from your package

• If your instructor must cancel with short notice, a complimentary make-up session will be provided

• Technical difficulties beyond our control do not result in class deductions

4. Class Validity & Expiration Timeline

To maintain learning momentum and ensure consistent progress, class packages have designated validity periods:

📅 5-class package: 30 days
📅 10-class package: 60 days
📅 15-class package: 90 days
📅 20-class package: 120 days
📅 30-class package: 150 days

Unused classes that exceed the validity period may be forfeited. We recommend maintaining regular sessions to maximize your investment.

💰 Refund & Discount Policy

Refund Guidelines

• Refund requests are honored within 7 days of your initial payment

• Discounted or promotional packages are generally non-refundable unless explicitly stated otherwise

• Approved refund requests are processed within 30 days and returned to your original payment method

• Classes already attended or consumed from your package cannot be refunded
